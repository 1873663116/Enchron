# Operation units

Generated by `Scripts/verification/journey_units.py reference`. Every unit below ends in a verdict you read before choosing the next one. Run them in the order their `needs` imply, not top to bottom.

38 units, 220 steps, 213 reachability cells.

`real` drives the product through its own hit testing. `injected` reaches past some of that path and says what it skips, so a pass there is worth less than a pass beside it. `setup` and `evidence` prove nothing on their own. `wearer` needs the human in the headset.

## playback.panorama

Enter panorama and come back.

- presentation: portal
- precondition: Portal presentation reached through the format editor.
- proves: Immersive entry settles and the window is not left resident.
- needs: playback.format-editor

1. `real` tap `PlayerUI-TopAction-resumePanorama`
   - expect: Immersive space opens and settles.
2. `evidence` assert `negative:immersive-resident-window` in panorama
   - expect: The main window is not in the hierarchy while immersive.
3. `injected` app `toggleControls` in panorama
   - expect: Controls appear over the immersive scene.
   - why injected: Panorama and Docked put the playback controls on a RealityKit collision body, and a synthetic touch never reaches one. Nothing in the app can be driven in those presentations until the controls are on screen.
   - skips: The summon gesture itself: hit test against the collision body and the tap gesture recogniser attached to it.
   - blind to: A regression that stops the video surface from accepting the summon tap looks identical to a pass at this step. The controls it reveals are still tapped for real afterwards.
4. `real` tap `PlayerPanel-menu-more` in panorama
   - expect: The panorama panel opens its more menu.
5. `real` tap `PlayerPanel-menu-audio` in panorama
   - expect: Audio list opens over the immersive scene.
6. `real` tap `PlayerPanel-menu-{category}-{item.id}` label FLAC in panorama
   - expect: Track changes without leaving panorama.
7. `real` tap `PlayerPanel-menu-subtitles` in panorama
   - expect: Subtitle list opens over the immersive scene.
8. `real` tap `PlayerPanel-menu-speed` in panorama
   - expect: Speed list opens over the immersive scene.
9. `real` tap `PlayerPanel-menu-episodes` in panorama
   - expect: Episode list opens over the immersive scene, or the control is absent for single-file media.
10. `real` tap `PlayerPanel-button-exit-spatial` in panorama
   - expect: Immersive space closes and the window returns.
